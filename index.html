02 / RESEARCH + DOCUMENTATION

Small rules.
Large consequences.

A visual field guide to the mechanism behind the models — and the boundary between an educational simulation and a living organism.

A / BIOLOGY

Cytoplasmic flow is the computation.

Physarum is a multinucleate plasmodium. Contractile tube walls drive rhythmic shuttle flow; nutrient-supported tubes carry more pressure-driven flow and strengthen, while low-use branches lose material.

conductance change ∝ flow − tube decay
B / MODEL

Explore first.

The simulated plasmodium begins with a low-conductance tube field over the available substrate. Pressure balance then reveals which connections can carry nutrient flow.

C / MODEL

Reinforce what works.

Each step solves for network pressure and adapts tube conductance to flow magnitude. This is the Physarum mechanism behind the route selection—not a genetic algorithm.

D / LIMITATION

Biologically inspired, not a lab.

The website is a deterministic teaching model. It reproduces the observed search–reinforcement–pruning story, not every molecular process inside a real culture.
